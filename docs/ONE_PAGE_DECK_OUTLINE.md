# One-Page Slide Deck Outline

## Slide 1 — Title
AI Code Quality Auditor
From research instrument to executive AI assurance platform

## Slide 2 — The problem
AI coding tools are being adopted quickly, but organizations lack a dependable way to evaluate their trustworthiness.
- Functional success is not enough
- Security, governance, and scope control matter
- Teams need evidence before adoption

## Slide 3 — The solution
We built a platform that evaluates AI coding tools against a fixed specification.
- evaluation engine for scoring and analysis
- executive dashboard for decision-friendly reporting
- partner deployment layer for pilots, demos, and enterprise validation

## Slide 4 — Why it matters
This shifts AI adoption from hype to evidence-based decision-making.
- reduces rollout risk
- supports governance, procurement, and pilot planning
- improves trust in AI-assisted engineering

## Slide 5 — What we have already achieved
- working evaluation engine and reporting workflow
- executive-ready dashboard for clearer interpretation
- pilot findings showing meaningful differences across tools
- a credible foundation for enterprise pilots and partner conversations

## Slide 6 — The market opportunity
This is relevant for:
- engineering leaders
- AI adoption teams
- risk and governance stakeholders
- enterprise partners evaluating AI tooling
- organizations moving from experimentation to governed rollout

## Slide 7 — The closing message
This is not another AI benchmark.
It is a practical assurance platform for trusted AI-assisted engineering.
It helps organizations decide whether an AI coding tool is safe, governable, and worthy of adoption.
